PHASE 61 — TERRITORIAL ECONOMY & PHYSICAL RESOURCE LOGISTICS

The economy is now tied to capturable industrial sites instead of abstract income.

RESOURCE/FACILITY POINTS
- Every strategic point is a facility: mine, oilfield, factory or depot.
- Capturing a point does not instantly pay the player.
- A captured site must be restarted by having an engineer at the site.
- Startup takes several seconds and is interrupted while contested or disabled.
- Once active, the site physically accumulates stock up to its storage limit.
- Facility level changes production capacity.
- Losing the point stops its production.

HELICOPTER LOGISTICS
- Logistics helicopters only fly to active facilities controlled by their team.
- A helicopter removes real stock from the facility when loading.
- The helicopter then physically carries that stock to a supply depot.
- Destroying the helicopter destroys the cargo it was carrying.
- Depot upgrades increase the number of simultaneous logistics helicopters.
- There is no fake income generated by the helicopter itself.

STRATEGIC AIRLIFT
- Cargo planes are an external supply line, separate from local resource sites.
- Cargo planes are unavailable until the airbase is upgraded.
- After the required airport upgrade, an external strategic stock pool accumulates.
- Cargo planes spawn from outside the map, carry a real cargo load, and must survive to land.
- Destroyed cargo planes lose their cargo.

TERRITORY
- Capturable points now have economic meaning in addition to area-control score.
- Minimap shows industrial sites and whether they are active.
- HUD shows controlled points, active sites, local stock and production rate.

SAVE
- Save format bumped to version 15.
- Facility ownership, stock, startup and production state are persisted.
- Logistics source assignment is persisted.
