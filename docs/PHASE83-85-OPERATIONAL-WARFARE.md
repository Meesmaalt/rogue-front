# Phase 83-85 — Operational Warfare

## Phase 83 — Strategic map layer
Bases, resource facilities and the centre corridor become operational sectors. Each sector tracks control, contest state, combat strength, threat and supply reach.

## Phase 84 — Operational AI
A commander layer sits above TacticalAI. It chooses an objective and allocates a reserve before sending a limited number of combat units toward the objective. TacticalAI still handles reconnaissance, artillery, air threat and resupply.

## Phase 85 — FOB / forward staging
Supply buildings upgraded into FOBs are registered as forward staging nodes. Their level determines operational radius and capacity. This creates a foundation for deployment zones and reinforcement routing.
