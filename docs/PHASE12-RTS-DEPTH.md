# Phase 12 – RTS depth pass

The Skirmish mode now models the core RTS loop more deeply:

- HQ start with engineers and two dedicated transport helicopters.
- Transport helicopters physically shuttle resource cargo from resource points back to their base.
- Resource points deplete and can become strategic objectives.
- Barracks, factory and helipad each have their own production queue.
- Infantry, engineers, tanks, artillery, transports, gunships and fighters occupy distinct production roles.
- Artillery has long range and splash damage.
- AA emplacements counter air units; bunkers provide static ground defence.
- Air research gates advanced air units.
- AI follows economy → base expansion → army production → attack rather than periodic waves.
- AI also builds logistics, air defence and artillery according to difficulty/personality.

The campaign objective system remains separate from Skirmish HQ-vs-HQ play.
