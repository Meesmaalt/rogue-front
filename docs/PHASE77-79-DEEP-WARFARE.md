# Rogue Front — Phase 77–79 Deep Warfare

## Phase 77 — Artillery & Counter-Battery
- Spotter/fresh-intel requirement
- Time-of-flight area fire
- Salvos and reload cycles
- Firing signatures
- Counter-battery response
- Automatic displacement after a dangerous salvo

## Phase 78 — Air Operations 2.0
- Sortie accounting
- AA threat state
- Return-to-base triggers
- SEAD / CAP / strike mission behavior
- Aircraft now have a more persistent operational cycle

## Phase 79 — Tactical AI
- Forward recon behavior
- Fire support based on fresh intel
- Counter-battery targeting
- Resupply/retreat behavior
- AA-aware aviation behavior
- Defensive posture when intelligence is stale

This phase deliberately avoids adding a separate disconnected subsystem: it connects existing Wargame sensors, logistics, combat, air doctrine and artillery into one tactical loop.
