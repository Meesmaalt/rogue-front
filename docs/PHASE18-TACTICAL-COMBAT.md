# Phase 18 — Tactical Combat

- Bunker and AA firing sectors are directional and follow building facing.
- AA prioritises aircraft inside its defended radius.
- Artillery can fire at long range when a friendly mobile unit provides spotting.
- Veteran units gain XP from kills and gain combat damage bonuses.
- AI uses transport helicopters for infantry drops when conditions allow.
- AI attack selection now prioritises economy and production infrastructure before HQ.
- Artillery stays behind the frontline during attacks.
- Tactical range rings and veterancy information are shown when a unit is selected.
- Save format is upgraded to v3 with compatibility for older saves.

Next: destructible walls/gates with dynamic pathfinding, dedicated artillery fire missions, scouting/fog improvements, supply pressure and deeper city combat.
