# Phase 23 – Base Economy & Expansion

## Start
- Skirmish starts with one HQ, engineers, infantry and two logistics helicopters.
- Logistics helicopters continuously collect from controlled resource points and return resources to the team pool.

## Construction
- Engineers construct buildings ordered from the base build panel.
- If no engineer is selected, the nearest available engineer is used.
- Construction is paid from the team resource/credit pools and is limited to the team base area.

## Base buildings
- Barracks – infantry and engineers.
- Vehicle Factory – tanks and artillery.
- Helicopter Base – helicopters and transport helicopters.
- Airfield – fighters, with rearm/refuel support.
- Refinery – economy/logistics hub.
- Supply Depot – forward supply hub.
- Radar Station – extended command/observation link.
- Bunker – fixed ground defence.
- AA – air defence.

## Air operations
- Fighters are produced at the Airfield.
- Helicopters remain tied to the Helicopter Base.
- Aircraft consume fuel/ammunition and return to an air facility for servicing.
