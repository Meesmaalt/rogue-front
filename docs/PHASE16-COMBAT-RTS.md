# Phase 16 — RTS Combat & Strategic Defense

- Team-specific technology trees: AI research no longer unlocks player air tech.
- Refinery income bonus is applied per completed refinery.
- Target selection now prioritizes air targets for missile weapons, structures for artillery/tanks, and HQs during sieges.
- Small-arms deal reduced damage to heavy structures.
- AI attack groups place artillery behind the armored spearhead and push through the enemy gate.
- All production queues now advance for both teams; AI can use the same production simulation.
- Save format v2 stores technology per team and remains compatible with v1 saves.
