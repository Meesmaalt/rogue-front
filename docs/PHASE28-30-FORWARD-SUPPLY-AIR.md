# Phase 28–30 — Forward Bases, Supply Lines & Air Operations

## Phase 28 — Forward Bases
- Supply depots can be placed outside the main-base radius.
- Generator/radar/helipad/bunker/AA may support forward positions.
- Supply depots form a chain from HQ; a depot is connected when within logistics range of an already connected HQ/depot.

## Phase 29 — Supply Lines
- Mobile units are supplied by the connected HQ/depot network rather than every building independently.
- Isolated forward units lose supply over time.
- Logistics helicopters collect map resources and deliver them to the nearest depot/HQ.
- Destroying a connecting depot can isolate a forward position.

## Phase 30 — Air Operations
- Aircraft produced at an airbase start grounded.
- Airbase capacity is limited.
- Aircraft use fuel/ammunition and return to their home base when low.
- Aircraft refuel, rearm and repair at helipads/airbases before becoming grounded/ready again.
- Fighters and gunships therefore behave as sorties instead of permanently airborne spawned units.
