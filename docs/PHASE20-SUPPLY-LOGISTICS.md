# Phase 20 – Supply, Logistics & Strategic Control

- Mobile units now carry a 0–100 supply state.
- HQ, refinery, barracks, factory and helipad act as supply hubs with different radii.
- Friendly transport helicopters can temporarily resupply nearby units.
- Units away from supply lose movement efficiency and combat output.
- Artillery consumes supply faster; air units consume it faster while operating away from base.
- Unit roles are explicit: line, support, siege, air-superiority, air-ground, logistics.
- AI can prioritize resource zones when no high-value enemy structure is visible.
- Save version 5 stores supply, role and remains backward compatible with previous saves.
