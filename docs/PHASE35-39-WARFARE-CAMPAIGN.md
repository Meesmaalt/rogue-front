# Phase 35–39 — Area Control, Amphibious Warfare, Morale & Mission Depth

## 35 — Area Control / Local Supply
- Resource points now behave as contested strategic control zones.
- Control produces strategic income and improves team morale.
- Contesting a zone drains morale over time.

## 36 — Amphibious Warfare
- Landing craft can load infantry, engineers and special forces.
- Landing craft can move to a target coast and unload a squad.

## 37 — Special Forces / Sabotage
- Special forces can disable enemy buildings temporarily.
- Generator/radar/airbase/shipyard sabotage also reduces the affected team's morale.

## 38 — Morale
- Team morale is affected by control and sabotage.
- Combat damage is scaled by morale, creating pressure to protect strategic areas.

## 39 — Mission Depth
- Added `capture` and `sabotage` objective types.
- Save version 9 stores morale, control and sabotage state.
- HUD exposes morale, controlled zones, air capacity and sabotage state.
