PHASE 83-85 — OPERATIONAL WARFARE BLOCK

Phase 83 — Strategic map layer
- Added persistent operational sectors derived from bases and resource facilities.
- Sectors track control, contest state, combat strength, threat and supply reach.
- Objectives are selected by strategic value and enemy pressure instead of nearest-unit distance.

Phase 84 — Operational AI
- Added OperationalCommander above the existing tactical AI.
- AI now maintains reserve, evaluates threat vs strength, selects sectors, attacks/defends/withdraws and sends groups toward objectives.
- Tactical AI remains responsible for individual-unit reactions.

Phase 85 — FOB / forward staging
- Added FOBManager for forward supply facilities.
- FOB level changes staging radius and capacity.
- Forward supply nodes are registered as operational staging areas and can be queried by future deployment UI.
