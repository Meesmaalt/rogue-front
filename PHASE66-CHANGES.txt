PHASE 66 — VISUAL MODEL OVERHAUL

Focus changed from new gameplay systems to model quality/readability.

Ground:
- More detailed tank, light tank, tank destroyer and SPAA silhouettes.
- Separate wheeled recon vehicle and tracked combat vehicle construction.
- Tracks/wheels now have visible hubs and running gear.
- Added optics, antennas, rear equipment and weapon details.
- Infantry, special forces and engineers are now small squads rather than one oversized soldier.
- APC/IFV/artillery/MLRS/logistics silhouettes remain distinct.

Air:
- Attack helicopter / transport helicopter / ECM / multirole silhouettes are separated.
- Aircraft receive canopies, intakes, pylons and sensor hints.

Buildings:
- HQ, barracks, factory, helipad, airbase, refinery, supply and radar receive additional structural details.
- Added windows, doors, rooftop equipment, crates, antennas and industrial fixtures.

Rendering:
- Existing GLB asset pipeline is preserved for future bespoke models.
- Procedural models remain the deterministic fallback and are the primary art-pass path.
