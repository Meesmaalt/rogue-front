Phase 59 — Wargame Combat Model
===============================

Goal:
- Make combat depend on platform characteristics instead of a single raw damage number.
- Preserve the Real War logistics layer from Phase 58.

Implemented:
- Per-platform accuracy profiles for infantry, armor, ATGM, artillery, air and naval units.
- Per-platform penetration values.
- Target-size modifier and range-based accuracy falloff.
- Stabilizer effect on accuracy while moving.
- Morale/suppression effects on accuracy.
- Veteran accuracy improvement.
- ECM disruption for guided/air weapons.
- Projectile-level hit chance resolved by the deterministic simulation RNG.
- Face-aware armor: front / side / rear values affect penetration and damage.
- Penetration threshold: under-penetrating hits deal substantially less damage; overmatch is rewarded.
- Temporary vehicle disablement on some penetrating hits.
- Disabled vehicles move slowly and cannot fire until the effect expires.
- Platform-specific reload skill affects practical rate of fire.
- HUD now exposes accuracy, penetration and reload factor for selected units.

Design direction for Phase 60:
- Physical logistics: ammunition/fuel depots, truck convoys, forward resupply, repair/rearm/refuel points.
- Supply routes and interdiction should become operational gameplay rather than passive regeneration.
