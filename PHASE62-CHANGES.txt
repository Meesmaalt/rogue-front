PHASE 62 – REAL WAR GROUND LOGISTICS / CONVOY NETWORK

Implemented:
- Physical road logistics using dedicated logiTruck units.
- Captured industrial/resource sites accumulate stock; trucks physically collect it.
- Trucks use the existing navigation/pathfinding and receive a road/bridge speed bonus.
- Resource -> forward supply depot -> trunk depot/HQ economic flow.
- A depot close to HQ is treated as the trunk depot; forward depots act as regional warehouses.
- Forward depot stock requires a second vulnerable road convoy to reach the trunk depot.
- Truck cargo is physical: destroyed trucks lose their current cargo.
- Road logistics statistics: active trucks, cargo in transit/storage, delivered and lost cargo, disconnected forward depots.
- Supply depot level increases storage capacity and convoy concurrency.
- Road convoy system runs for both player and AI.
- Dedicated visual silhouette for MTVR-style logistics trucks.
- Save state upgraded to v16 with backward loading support for v15 and earlier.
- HUD now exposes ground convoy/logistics status and selected truck cargo/phase.
- Existing helicopter airbridge remains in parallel: road convoys are the cheaper baseline, helicopters are faster but exposed to AA/air defense.
- Existing strategic cargo-plane system remains a separate late-game airlift layer.

Design principle:
RESOURCE SITE -> TRUCK -> FORWARD DEPOT -> TRUCK -> TRUNK DEPOT/HQ -> SPENDABLE RESOURCES

Risk model:
- Longer routes mean longer exposure time.
- Road/bridge movement is faster, so road control matters.
- Convoys are real entities and can be detected and destroyed.
- Lost truck cargo is not refunded.
